Roster and wages system

Each store sets the week's sales forecast, taken from the average of its recent weeks' actual sales, and a wage cap. Generate roster then fills the whole week inside that cap. Managers adjust it on the grid, and head office sees every store before payroll.

  • Learns a store's preferred shift pattern and follows it next time
  • Keeps a rest gap between a late close and an early start
  • Labour % against the cap updates as shifts change
  • Replaced a paid per-seat rostering tool

Results

What the work has Delivered

A five-figure royalty recovery

Recovered a five-figure royalty overcharge by rebuilding three years of royalty history line by line. The refund came back with a renegotiated, market-based currency conversion rate that keeps cutting ongoing costs.

Supplier overcharges recovered

Recovered supplier overcharges across the network. Caught a supplier charging above the agreed cost price and tightened reconciliation controls so it can't recur.

About 3% higher gross margin

Built food-cost structures and menu and inventory pricing with operations, so pricing follows cost instead of instinct.

Paid tools replaced in-house

Replaced a paid per-seat rostering tool and several disconnected systems, and took one business off Xero. Comparable tools would cost roughly NZD 37,000 or more a year to buy as off-the-shelf subscriptions (estimate from vendors' public pricing, Oct 2026). Month-end reporting also closes about 2 days sooner.

Commercial work

Two franchisee defaults, handled end to end

Moved one franchisee to cash-on-purchase supply. Suspended supply to the other and instructed lawyers on franchise termination.

Two commercial leases negotiated

Secured fit-out removal rights and changed commencement dates to match store opening plans.

Guarantee instead of a cash deposit

Arranged a bank guarantee in place of a landlord deposit for a new store. Prepared the group portfolio for a revolving credit facility.

Australian joint venture model

Modelled a 70/30 joint venture to take a group company into Australia, covering capital, cost split and ownership.

Serviced-office takeover

Took over a commercial serviced-office property, restructured its expense payments and audited tenant records after the handover.

Supplier tenders and fair cost allocation

Compared local and overseas packaging and ran a pantry supplier tender. Designed a monthly method to bill shared digital marketing to each branch.

Names and figures of counterparties are left out.
